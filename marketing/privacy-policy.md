# Privacy Policy

**Mermaid Live Diagrams for Confluence**

Last updated: 19 September 2026

## The short version

This app collects nothing, stores nothing outside your Confluence site, and
sends nothing to any server we run. We do not run a server.

## What the app does with your data

Your diagram source, caption and display settings are saved as Confluence
macro parameters. They live inside your own Confluence page, under Atlassian's
control and your site's own permissions. We never receive a copy.

Diagrams are drawn in your browser. The Mermaid library is bundled into the
app and served from Atlassian's hosting, so rendering a diagram makes no
request to any third party.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no backend, no analytics, no telemetry, no cookies and no
logging of your content. We cannot see your diagrams, your pages, your site or
who you are.

## Permissions

The app requests no Confluence API scopes. It reads no pages, no spaces, no
user profiles and no attachments. Its only access is to the settings you type
into its own macro.

## Third parties

There are none. Nothing about your use of this app is shared with anyone,
because nothing is collected.

The app blocks outbound requests from diagram content as a matter of design:
markup that would load a remote image, script, stylesheet or frame is removed
from your diagram source before it is rendered, and again from the rendered
output. A diagram cannot be used to place a tracking pixel on your page.

## Atlassian

The app runs on Atlassian's Forge platform. Atlassian hosts the app's files
and stores the macro settings alongside your page content. Atlassian's own
privacy policy governs that data: https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy

## Data deletion

Uninstalling the app removes it from your site. The macro settings are part of
your page content and are removed when you delete the macro or the page. There
is nothing held anywhere else to delete.

## Changes

Any change to this policy will be published here with a new date, and
described in the app's release notes.
